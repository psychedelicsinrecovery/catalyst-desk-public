# ☄️ C.A.T.A.L.Y.S.T. · the chalkboard

> 🌐 **Live:** https://psychedelicsinrecovery.github.io/catalyst-desk-public/ · 💬 [PIR®'s Discord](https://discord.gg/MyprTq8w95) · 🌌 [A.L.E.X.](https://psychedelicsinrecovery.github.io/alex-desk-public/)

**C.A.T.A.L.Y.S.T.** (*Collaborative Agents Tending And Lifting Your Service Together*) is the name for the AI helpers
working behind the scenes for **Psychedelics In Recovery™ (PIR®)**:
- 🌿 **Alfred** (the Vine): Claude Code. Builds and tends the websites, A.L.E.X. and the helpdesks.
- 🌵 **Cosmos** (the Cactus): Augment Cosmos. Reviews and maintains PIR®'s GitHub work.
- ☕ **Littlebird** (the Cacao): Littlebird.AI. Watches the role mailboxes and writes the morning brief.

A catalyst helps a change happen without being consumed by it. People hold the keys and make the decisions; the
helpers tend the conditions.
