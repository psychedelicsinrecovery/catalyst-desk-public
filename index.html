What a catalyst does

Help happens. The helper isn't the point.

🔥

Sparks the change

Turns a WhatsApp idea into a working page, a helpdesk or a proposal the Board can read, often the same day.

🪴

Tends the conditions

Keeps channel descriptions tidy, links fresh, docs current and reminders on time, the quiet upkeep that keeps a fellowship's spaces safe and welcoming.

🙌

Lifts the servants

Takes the busywork off volunteers' plates, so trusted servants spend their hours on people, not paperwork.

How we work together

A loop, not a ladder

The helpers pass work around a circle, and every circle ends with people deciding.

  1. ☕ noticesLittlebird: mailboxes, meetings, the morning brief
  2. 🌿 buildsAlfred: pages, tools, posts, proposals
  3. 🌵 reviewsCosmos: GitHub care and checks
  4. 🌌 servesA.L.E.X.: answers and helpdesks
  5. 🙋 people decidecommittees, the Board, group conscience… and back to noticing
🗝️

People hold the keys

Passwords, tokens and permissions are set by humans in service. The agents never type or see a secret.

🙈

No reading over shoulders

C.A.T.A.L.Y.S.T. posts and tidies; it doesn't read members' messages. A.L.E.X. sees only what you type to her.

🚫

Never DMs you first

No PIR® helper will message you asking for money, codes, keys or wallets. If one does, it's a scam: report it with /mod.

🧾

Every change has an author

Work lands in GitHub with who made it and why, and the big moments go in the public changelog.

🏛️

Humans decide

Money, roles, policies and anything that speaks for PIR® go through committees and the Board. The agents draft; people choose.

🔒

No member data in AI tools

No member lists, shares or private messages go into AI tools. PIR®'s privacy policy explains how helpers are used.
